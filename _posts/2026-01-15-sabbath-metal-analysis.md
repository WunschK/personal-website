A fun computational project: did Tony Iommi really write every riff?

## The Question

There's a running joke in metal circles: "Every riff was already written by Tony Iommi." But can we test this computationally?

## The Approach

Using audio analysis and pattern matching:
1. Extract riff patterns from Black Sabbath songs
2. Compare them to a database of metal songs
3. Identify similarities and influences
4. Measure the "Sabbath-ness" of any riff

## Challenges

- Defining what makes a riff similar
- Accounting for different tunings and tempos
- Distinguishing influence from coincidence
- Respecting copyright and fair use

## Status

This is a side project for fun, combining music theory, digital signal processing, and historical analysis. Stay tuned for updates!
