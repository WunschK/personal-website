My PhD project: creating a digital edition of Aurora von Königsmarck's correspondence and life.

## Who Was Aurora?

Aurora von Königsmarck (1662-1728) was a remarkable figure: noblewoman, intellectual, and influential correspondent in early Enlightenment Europe.

## Project Goals

- Critical edition of her correspondence
- Biographical research
- Network analysis of her relationships
- Digital presentation for scholars and public

## Methodology

Combining traditional philological methods with digital humanities approaches:
- TEI encoding of letters
- Prosopographical database
- Network visualization
- Geographic mapping

## Status

Currently in active development. Both a project website and digital edition site are planned.
