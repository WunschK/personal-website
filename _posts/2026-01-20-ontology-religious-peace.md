Building structured semantic models for historical peace agreements.

## Overview

Religious peace agreements have played a crucial role throughout European history. By developing an ontology for these agreements, we can create a structured framework for understanding their relationships, patterns, and historical significance.

## Key Challenges

- Defining consistent categories across different time periods
- Representing complex political and religious relationships
- Linking agreements to their historical context
- Enabling computational analysis while preserving nuance

## Applications

This ontology enables scholars to:
- Query agreements by theological concepts
- Identify patterns across centuries
- Link related documents automatically
- Generate visualizations of historical networks
