Welcome to my blog! This is where I share my thoughts on digital humanities, programming, and technology.

## About This Blog

This blog is built using Jekyll and GitHub Pages, combining modern web development practices with a clean, custom design. It's a space where I can explore ideas at the intersection of history, technology, and digital scholarship.

## What You'll Find Here

### Digital Humanities
I'll be writing about:
- Retrieval Augmented Generation (RAG) in digital editions
- Ontologies and structured data in historical research
- The role of LLMs and AI in historical analysis
- Digital edition design and implementation

### Web Development
Thoughts on:
- Responsive design principles
- Modern CSS and JavaScript techniques
- Static site generation with Jekyll
- Building accessible web experiences

### Research Projects
Updates on my current work, including:
- My PhD project on digital editions
- Collaborative research initiatives
- Open source contributions to DH tools
