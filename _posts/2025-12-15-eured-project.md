A comprehensive digital resource for European religious peace agreements.

## About EuReD

The project digitizes and analyzes religious peace agreements from the Reformation era, making them accessible for research and teaching.

## My Role

- Frontend development and design system
- User experience design
- Responsive implementation
- Accessibility consulting

## Features

- Critical editions of historical texts
- Multi-language support
- Advanced search capabilities
- Semantic annotation
- Citation tools

## Impact

The platform serves researchers across Europe, enabling new forms of comparative analysis of religious peace agreements.
