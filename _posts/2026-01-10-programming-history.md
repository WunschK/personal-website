Examining programming itself as a historical practice and method.

## History of Programming

Programming has its own rich history worth exploring:
- Evolution of languages and paradigms
- Social history of computing communities
- Gender and computing
- Open source movements

## Programming AS Historical Method

But more importantly: how does programming change historical research?
- Enables new types of questions
- Scales analysis beyond human reading
- Makes arguments reproducible
- Creates new forms of publication

## Theoretical Questions

- Is code interpretation or data?
- How do we cite computational methods?
- What is the epistemology of algorithmic history?
