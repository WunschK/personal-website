Contributing to Text+, the German research data infrastructure for text-based research.

## What is Text+?

Text+ is part of Germany's National Research Data Infrastructure (NFDI), focused on providing services and standards for text-based research across disciplines.

## My Involvement

Working on aspects of:
- Data standards and best practices
- Tool development and integration
- Community building
- Training and documentation

## Why It Matters

Research infrastructure is crucial for:
- Long-term preservation of research data
- Interoperability between projects
- Reproducibility of research
- Lowering barriers for digital methods

## Goals

Making digital humanities tools and methods accessible to all researchers, regardless of technical background.
