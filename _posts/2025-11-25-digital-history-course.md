Teaching a course on how digital methods are transforming historical research.

## Course Overview

"Digital History: Update der Geschichtswissenschaft" introduces students to digital approaches in historical research.

## Topics Covered

- Digital archives and sources
- Text analysis and distant reading
- Network analysis for historical relationships
- Mapping and spatial history
- Database design for historical data
- Digital editions and publication

## Pedagogical Approach

Hands-on learning with:
- Real historical sources
- Open-source tools
- Group projects
- Critical reflection on methods

## Goals

Students learn both practical skills and theoretical understanding of how digital methods change historical inquiry.
