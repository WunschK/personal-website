Creating a digital edition of the accounts of George Anson's 18th-century voyage around the world.

## The Project

George Anson's circumnavigation (1740-1744) was one of the most significant naval expeditions of the 18th century. This project creates a digital scholarly edition of the various accounts of the voyage.

## What Makes It Digital?

- **Multiple versions**: Compare different editions and translations
- **Annotations**: Link to historical context, places, persons
- **Visualizations**: Maps of the route, timeline of events
- **Searchable**: Full-text search across all documents
- **Linked data**: Connect to other historical resources

## Technical Approach

- TEI XML encoding for texts
- Geographic coordinates for places
- Prosopographical database for persons
- Interactive web interface
