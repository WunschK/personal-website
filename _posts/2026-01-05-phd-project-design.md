Designing both a digital edition website and a project website for my PhD research.

## The Dual Challenge

My PhD project required two distinct websites:

### 1. The Digital Edition
A scholarly resource for primary sources:
- Critical apparatus and annotations
- Search and browse functionality
- Citation mechanisms
- Long-term preservation considerations

### 2. The Project Website
Public-facing information:
- Project goals and team
- News and updates
- Publications and outputs
- Contact and collaboration opportunities

## Design Philosophy

Both sites share a visual language but serve different audiences. The edition prioritizes functionality for researchers, while the project site emphasizes clarity for general visitors.

## Technical Decisions

- Static site generation for longevity
- Standards-compliant TEI encoding
- Progressive enhancement
- Mobile-first responsive design
