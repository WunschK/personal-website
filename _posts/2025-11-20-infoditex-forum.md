Organizing and leading an interdisciplinary forum for digital text scholarship since 2020.

## About InFoDiTex

The Interdisciplinary Forum for Digital Textual Sciences brings together scholars working with digital text methods across disciplines.

## My Role

Since 2020, I've organized InFoDiTex:
- First with Christopher Nunn (Heidelberg)
- Then with Melanie Seltmann (Berlin)
- Currently with Fernanda Alvares Freire (Darmstadt)

## Activities

- Regular workshops and presentations
- Tool demonstrations
- Project showcases
- Networking events
- Methodological discussions

## Community

We bring together:
- Literary scholars
- Historians
- Linguists
- Computer scientists
- Information scientists

## Impact

InFoDiTex creates a space for cross-disciplinary exchange, helping researchers learn from each other's approaches and tools.
