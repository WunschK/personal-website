Exploring how large language models are transforming historical scholarship.

## The Promise

LLMs offer exciting possibilities for historians:
- Automated transcription and translation
- Pattern recognition across large corpora
- Research assistance and source discovery
- Accessibility for non-specialist audiences

## The Risks

However, critical concerns remain:
- **Hallucinations**: Models can invent plausible-sounding but false information
- **Bias**: Training data reflects historical biases that can be amplified
- **Context**: AI often misses crucial historical context
- **Authority**: Questions about scholarly interpretation vs. automated analysis

## A Balanced Approach

The key is using AI as a tool to augment, not replace, scholarly expertise. Transparency and verification remain essential.
